My Favourite City: Montréal

Montréal is a city in Québec, Canada, known for its vibrant arts scene, diverse neighbourhoods, and food.

The city combines historic streets with modern attractions. It is especially enjoyable to explore on foot, and its festivals are worth experiencing.

Five Popular Attractions

  • Mount Royal Park

  • Montréal Botanical Garden
  • Jean-Talon Market

Five Things to Do

  1. Walk through Old Montréal.
  2. Enjoy the view from Mount Royal.
  3. Try a Montréal-style bagel.
  4. Visit a local museum.
  5. Explore a public market.
